Starkpad

ARDUINO UNO Q · YOCTO LINUX · LVGL · SHIDP · XIAO RP2040 · TINYUSB

A Linux-powered touchscreen macro deck. Arduino UNO Q — Qualcomm Dragonwing QRB2210 with an STM32U585 bridge — running Yocto Linux, UI in LVGL, talking to the host over SHIDP, a custom serial HID protocol, through a XIAO RP2040 dongle on TinyUSB. A 60-button grid, virtual keyboard, precision touchpad, web-based configuration.

Featured by Arduino, Hackster.io and LVGL.
Defended as bachelor thesis — June 8, 2026.
